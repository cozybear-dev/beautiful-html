One phished mailbox became domain admin in 41 hours

Nothing in the path was an exploit. Every step used something that was already allowed.

Illustrative scenario for a tabletop exercise. Names and timings are invented.

Each step used access the last one had earned

The intruder never broke in twice. A stolen session opened a mailbox, the mailbox delivered a document, and each machine handed over the credential for the next.

Step through the path, or press Play.

Thirty of the 41 hours passed between two machines

The intruder waited on the first workstation until a file-server administrator signed in. Nothing was watching for it.

Any one of three controls would have ended it

  1. Bind sessions to the device

    The stolen mail session would not have worked from the intruder's machine.

  2. Keep administrators off workstations

    There would have been no file-server credential to wait for.

  3. Alert on new service tickets for old accounts

    The last hop would have paged someone within minutes.
