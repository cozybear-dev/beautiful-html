Hunt & HackettThreat landscape, third quarter 2026

Intruders now act within days, not weeks

Hunt & Hackett Threat Intelligence, 10 October 2026. Based on 214 intrusions investigated since January 2023.

Every sector shortened, and logistics is still the slowest to be hit

The fall is not one group changing tactics. It shows in every sector we respond in, on the same scale. Logistics firms still get the most warning, because their networks are large and flat and take longer to map.

Four in ten intrusions began with a password that worked

No exploit, no malware: the intruder signed in. Most of those passwords came from an earlier breach somewhere else, or from a session stolen through a relay page.

What counts as valid credentials

A password, token or session that the system accepted as genuine. A password guessed against an exposed service is counted under that service.

Once inside, they go for credentials before anything else

Read across a row to see what an intrusion in that sector usually involves. Credential access stands out in every row: it is how one foothold becomes many. Exfiltration is what separates the sectors, and impact is rarest where data is worth more than downtime.

Cases where the first alert was read within a day stayed small

In all but one of the cases an alert had fired before the damage. What differed was how long it sat unread. Each dot is a case from the last twelve months.

Four days is shorter than most alert queues

A detection only helps if someone reads it in time. Most alerts close without a person looking; the ones that matter are a thin stream.

If the queue takes longer than four days to reach an alert, the alert arrives after the damage.

Method

Figures come from intrusions Hunt & Hackett investigated between January 2023 and September 2026. "Impact" is the first encryption, destruction or confirmed theft of data. Cases stopped before impact are left out of the time to impact, and included everywhere else.

Hunt & Hackett, 10 October 2026. An example publication: every figure in it is invented.
