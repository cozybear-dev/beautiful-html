Usage grew fastest in the three months after onboarding

Monthly active users were flat at around 320 through the winter. The onboarding programme ran in May 2026, and active use has risen every month since. Sessions per user rose too, from 17 to 21 a month, so people are not only signing in but coming back.

How we count

An active user signed in and completed at least one action in the calendar month. Service accounts are excluded.

Warehouse Ops and Finance account for most unused seats

Five of the seven teams have more than 70% of their seats in weekly use. Warehouse Ops and Finance are the exception. Together they hold 214 seats and use 86.

Recommendation

Run the onboarding programme for Warehouse Ops and Finance in November

The same two-week programme lifted weekly use in Dispatch from 61% to 91%. If it does half as well here, about 60 more seats come into use before the December renewal.

Support is faster, and satisfaction followed

Median first response fell from 9.2 hours to 2.6 after we assigned Northwind a named support engineer in April. Satisfaction rose from 84% to 95% over the same months.
