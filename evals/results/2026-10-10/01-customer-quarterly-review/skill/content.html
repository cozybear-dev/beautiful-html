More of your people use Routewise, and they use it more often

Monthly active users were flat at 300 to 330 from October to April. The onboarding programme ran in May 2026, and active use has risen every month since: 474 in July, 514 in August, 538 in September. That is 206 more people than in April.

Monthly and weekly

This section counts people active at least once in a calendar month. The team figures further down count people active in a week, which is a stricter test and gives lower numbers.

The new users are not signing in once and leaving. Sessions per active user rose from 17 a month in April to 21 in June and stayed there through the quarter, so total sessions grew 36% on Q2, to 32,394. API calls reached 962,000 in the quarter, up 16% on Q2. They were already growing before May, so we do not credit that rise to onboarding.

Warehouse Ops and Finance are not yet getting value from their seats

Five of the seven teams have more than 70% of their seats in use each week. Warehouse Ops and Finance do not. Together they hold 214 seats, a third of the contract, and use 85 of them in a typical week. That is 129 of the 199 seats that go unused each week.

The same two teams have barely started with Route Planner: 18% adoption in Warehouse Ops and 11% in Finance, against 78% in Dispatch. Customer Service is a second, smaller gap. Its seats are well used, but only about half the team has adopted Route Planner.

What we cannot show

We hold team figures for September only, so this report cannot say how each team moved after the May programme. We will track teams monthly from October.

Support answers in under three hours, and satisfaction has followed

Median first response fell from 9.0 hours in March to 5.1 in June and 2.6 in September. Satisfaction rose from 83% to 90% to 95% over the same months.

Your teams opened 194 tickets in Q3, against 174 in Q2, and we resolved 201. More people using the product means more questions, but fewer per person: about 13 tickets per 100 active users a month in Q3, down from 15 in Q1.

We propose to onboard Warehouse Ops and Finance before the renewal

Proposal

Run the onboarding programme for Warehouse Ops and Finance in November

The May programme was followed by 206 more monthly active users in five months. If these two teams reached 73% weekly use, the level of the lowest of the other five teams, about 70 more seats would be in use each week.

Onboard two teams

The May programme, adapted to warehouse shifts and to finance tasks. We need a sponsor in each team to make it stick.

Check the seats fit the roles

Before training, agree with each team lead which of the 214 seats belong to people who need Routewise for their work.

Extend Route Planner

A short Route Planner session for Customer Service, where 52% have adopted it.
