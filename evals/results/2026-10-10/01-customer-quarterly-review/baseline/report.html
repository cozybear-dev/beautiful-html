Quarterly business review

Northwind Logistics, Q3 2026

July to September 2026 · Prepared by Routewise Customer Success for the review meeting on 21 October 2026

More of Northwind used Routewise in Q3, and used it more often. 538 of your 650 licensed seats were active in September, up from 446 in June and 332 in April, before the onboarding programme. Support also got faster: median first response fell from 5.1 hours in June to 2.6 in September, and satisfaction reached 95%.

Two teams are not yet getting that value. Warehouse Ops and Finance hold 214 seats between them and 85 are active week to week. Their Route Planner adoption is 18% and 11%, against 78% in Dispatch.

We propose to spend Q4 on those two teams, and to agree with you how seats are allocated by team before the contract renews in December.

Active users were flat at 300 to 330 until April. They rose to 415 in May, the month of the onboarding programme, and have grown every month since. September left 112 seats without an active user, compared with 318 in April.

People are also doing more in the product. Each active user averaged 21.2 sessions a month in Q3, against 17.0 in the seven months before the programme. API calls totalled 962,459 for the quarter, 16% more than Q2. They dipped to 306K in August before reaching a 12-month high of 333K in September.

Support

Median first response is now under 3 hours, and satisfaction is at 95%

October 2025 to September 2026. The shaded area is Q3 2026.

Your teams opened 194 tickets in Q3, up from 174 in Q2. That is what we would expect with more people using the product: per 100 active users, tickets fell from 14.6 a month to 12.7. We resolved 201 tickets in the quarter, so the open backlog went down.

Median first response was 9 hours in March and 2.6 hours in both August and September. Satisfaction moved from 83% to 95% over the same period.

Where value is not landing yet

Warehouse Ops and Finance account for 129 of the 199 seats that are not active week to week

Five of the seven teams have between 73% and 91% of their seats active every week. Warehouse Ops (61 of 150) and Finance (24 of 64) are at about 40%. Warehouse Ops is your largest team by seats, so it is also where the most unused capacity sits.

Route Planner follows a similar pattern. It is well established in Dispatch (78%) and Fleet Planning (71%). Customer Service is an active team, with 95 of 120 seats in use each week, but only 52% Route Planner adoption.

Team figures count weekly active users, a stricter measure than the monthly count in the first section. That is why they total 451 rather than 538.

What we propose for Q4

Focus on the two low-usage teams, then set seat allocation before renewal

1

Onboard Warehouse Ops and Finance around their own work

We start with a working session with each team lead to identify the tasks that belong in Routewise, then run onboarding built on those tasks.

From Northwind: a named lead in each team and time for their people to attend.

2

Find out where Route Planner fits in Customer Service

We sit with the Customer Service lead to see where planning fits the team's work, and run a Route Planner session if it does.

From Northwind: an hour with the Customer Service lead.

3

Review seats by team ahead of the December renewal

We bring updated team figures in November, so the renewal is based on where seats are used after the Q4 work.

From Northwind: a date in November for that review.

We will report the same team figures at the next quarterly review, so you can see whether this worked.

For 21 October

Three things to agree in the meeting

  1. Who leads the onboarding work in Warehouse Ops and in Finance.
  2. Whether to go ahead with the Route Planner review in Customer Service.
  3. A date in November to review seat allocation by team before renewal.
