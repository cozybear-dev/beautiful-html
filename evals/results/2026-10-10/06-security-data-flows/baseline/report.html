How customer data flows through our systems

Prepared for your vendor security review · 10 October 2026

Customer data enters through a CDN and web application firewall, passes a load balancer into our application services, and is stored in 4 data stores that are encrypted at rest with AES-256. All production systems run in the EU (Frankfurt and Dublin).

16 of the 17 connections between systems are encrypted with TLS 1.2 or 1.3. The exception is a nightly export of pseudonymised data from the primary database to the reporting warehouse, which uses unencrypted FTP inside our private network. It is described in full directly below and is due to be replaced by 30 November 2026.

1. Known gap: unencrypted nightly export to the reporting warehouse

Connection 17, Primary database to Reporting warehouse, is not encrypted in transit. Once a night, a pseudonymised export is copied from the primary database to the reporting warehouse over FTP (port 21), authenticated with a shared password. FTP does not encrypt, so both the export and that password cross the private network in clear text. Both systems are in the Data zone, and the connection does not leave the private network.

Identity provider

Sign-in is delegated to an identity provider operated by the customer or by Okta (connection 5). The web application exchanges SAML / OIDC sign-in messages with it over TLS 1.3, authenticated by signed assertions. Credentials are held by the identity provider.

6. Controls and assurance

Encryption at rest
AES-256 for the primary database, document storage, search index and reporting warehouse; keys in a managed KMS, rotated yearly.
Encryption in transit
TLS 1.2 or 1.3 on 16 of 17 connections; see section 1 for the exception.
Data residency
All production systems run in the EU (Frankfurt and Dublin). The email provider processes in the EU; the log platform in the EU.
